I am an Associate Professor in the [School of Computer and Communication Engineering](https://scce.ustb.edu.cn/) at the [University of Science and Technology Beijing (USTB)](https://www.ustb.edu.cn/), and a master's supervisor in computer architecture. I have worked on wireless sensing, swarm robotics, and embedded computing since 2005. In 2014–2015 I was a visiting researcher in Prof. [Kaveh Pahlavan](https://www.wpi.edu/)'s laboratory at Worcester Polytechnic Institute, working on frequency-domain wireless measurement and channel analysis.

My current work focuses on **multi-agent reinforcement learning**, **cooperative localization and target tracking**, and **near-ground swarm-robot communication**. Representative results appear in IEEE TPAMI, IEEE TSMC, IEEE JSAC, IEEE Transactions on Wireless Communications, and IEEE Internet of Things Journal. Two papers were accepted to **IROS 2026** (corresponding author).

Research
======
- **Multi-agent reinforcement learning.** Dynamic deep factor graphs, subgoal-based hierarchical collaboration, and credit assignment for cooperative control.
- **Swarm-robot localization and tracking.** RL-compensated filtering, self-attention factor-graph networks, constrained Gaussian condensation, and federated cooperative localization.
- **Near-ground wireless communication.** UWB channel measurement and piecewise near-ground models for swarm robots; industrial wireless reliability (WIA-PA / WIA-FA).
- **WiFi / wearable sensing.** CSI-based driver activity recognition (WiDriver) and deep models for complex human activity (InnoHAR).
- **Sim2Real and field systems.** Domain adaptation for robot navigation, plus embedded systems for heat metering, pipeline monitoring, and instrument recognition.
