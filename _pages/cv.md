Education
======

Work experience
======
* 1998 – present: Faculty member, Department of Computer Science and Technology / School of Computer and Communication Engineering, University of Science and Technology Beijing
  * Associate Professor and master's supervisor
  * Research: multi-agent systems, swarm robotics, wireless localization, and embedded computing
* 2014 – 2015: Visiting researcher
  * Worcester Polytechnic Institute (WPI)
  * Wireless signal measurement and channel analysis on a frequency-domain platform
  * Host: Prof. Kaveh Pahlavan

Research interests
======
* Multi-agent reinforcement learning and factor-graph methods
* Cooperative localization, target tracking, and SLAM exploration
* Near-ground UWB / industrial wireless communication
* WiFi CSI and wearable sensing for activity recognition
* Embedded computing for engineering systems

Selected projects
======

* Participant in NSFC, National 863, and industrial wireless standard efforts (WIA-PA, WIA-FA)
* Engineering systems: heat-metering control, petroleum-pipeline remote monitoring, pointer-instrument recognition, and cathodic-protection visualization
